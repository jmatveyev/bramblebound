Bramblebound

A world built for wandering.

Play as Nori, a fox courier whose sleepy village has misplaced the morning. Explore Hearthgrove and its neighboring wilds, find the three old bells, and discover what has taken root beneath the great tree.

This is an original, compact action-adventure. Its places, characters, story, models, music, and puzzles were created for this game. It blends readable close-range combat, expressive jumping, and a world that opens up as you discover new abilities.

There is no timer, no stamina meter, and no penalty to your collection when you fall. Lanterns restore your health. You can keep exploring after the ending.

Requires a browser with WebGL 2. Everything is contained in this file. No account, payment, external asset requests, or mouse capture. Music begins only after you start. Browser save storage may be unavailable in some previews or private windows; use Export save as a backup.

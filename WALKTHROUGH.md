# BRAMBLEBOUND: optional route guide

This file contains spoilers. The in-game atlas offers one contextual hint at a
time through **A nudge in the right direction**.

## Hearthgrove and the sunring

Speak with Moss near the village square. Stand south of the workshop crate,
hold E, and move north until it reaches the brass floor circle. Once the gate
opens, walk through the west entrance and use E on the chest. The sunring is a
returning throwing weapon. The village's east arch now opens to Brookfall.

## Brookfall and the River Bell

Activate all three blue ring targets by facing them and throwing Q from within
range. One is on the western bank, one on the eastern bank, and one at the
northern shrine. Islands, stepping stones, and a moving platform connect the
routes. The far shrine target can also be hit from the north end of the east
bank before the bridge opens.

The three targets raise the bridge north of the mill. Jump onto it, cross, jump
onto the shrine, and ring the bell with E. Windstep grants a second airborne
jump. Use the arch behind the shrine to return home.

Optional: explore behind the western waterfall for the Hidden Hollow. Its gold
mushroom launches you toward the ledges, treasure, and a star moth.

## Sunstep Ruins and the Sky Bell

Enter through Hearthgrove's west arch. The three terraces each hold a glowing
wind spindle: lower west, middle east, and upper west. Follow stepping stones
up from the starting terrace. Board the first moving platform when it approaches
the western edge and ride it toward the eastern terrace. Double-jump from it.

At the second crossing, board the higher moving platform from the east and ride
west. Touch the lantern on the upper terrace. Collect its spindle, then use the
last stepping stone and a double jump to reach the summit. Do not try to clear
the entire moving-platform crossing in one jump. A late second jump is useful.

Ring the summit bell. The Sunlit Blade now lets charged spinning attacks cut
dark thorn barriers. The summit arch returns to Hearthgrove.

## Moonroot Sanctuary and the Moon Bell

Use the northern village arch. Hold J long enough to charge, then release beside
the dark thorns. Walk around the sanctuary and turn its three prisms with E.
Each interaction rotates a prism one quarter turn clockwise.

The beam should travel:

1. Western prism: EAST.
2. Middle-right prism: NORTH.
3. Top-right prism: WEST.

The visible light path confirms the solution. Reach the raised western shrine,
ring the final bell, and return through its arch.

## The Hollow Crown

All three bells open the northeastern village arch. Move into the arena to wake
the Briar Warden. Watch its attack cues rather than trying to trade damage.

Stay several steps away during its ground attacks. Jump when an expanding shock
ring reaches you. Later phases send additional rings. Walk out of marked root
circles before they erupt. When the eyes shine gold, throw the sunring to stun
the guardian. Run close and attack the exposed core with the blade or a spin.

The guardian limits damage per opening, so repeat the sequence across its three
phases. After the ending, return to Hearthgrove to find remaining secrets.

## Optional discoveries

Six star moths are hidden across Hearthgrove, Brookfall, Hidden Hollow, Sunstep,
and Moonroot. Pip in the village rewards all six with an extra heart. Treasure
chests, breakable pots, bushes, and ordinary path acorns fund Toma's upgrades.
Health vessels add hearts; the traveler's sash improves speed; sunring lining
increases the weapon's range and damage. The atlas tracks discoveries.
